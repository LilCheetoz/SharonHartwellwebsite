Notes & Rationale
  • Teal primary — not associated with any BC party. Reads as governmental and trustworthy.
  • BC Gold retained — anchors the site to the provincial legislature identity without partisan associations.
  • No red, no strong Conservative blue — avoids party colour associations entirely.
  • Charcoal with teal tint — dark mode feels calm and professional, not harsh.
  • Lora for headings — replaces Playfair Display with something slightly warmer and less editorial.
  • Inter for everything else — highly legible at all sizes, excellent for forms, navigation, and body text.
  • Both fonts load from Google Fonts; no licence cost or hosting required.
