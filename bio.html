Her Story

A Dedicated Advocate for Bulkley Valley-Stikine

Sharon is deeply rooted in the Bulkley Valley, a place she proudly calls home. Born and raised in this picturesque rural area, she has not only built her life here but has also instilled her values of community engagement and growth in her two sons, who have chosen to remain in the same region.

From an early age, Sharon was raised to be community-minded and to actively participate in shaping the future of her community and its surroundings. As a business owner, she recognized the need to showcase the beauty and potential of the region — promoting its natural assets and economic opportunities while encouraging business growth.

Local Leadership: Telkwa Council & Mayor

Sharon's dedication to community led her to run for Village Council in Telkwa, where she served a term and a half. Her desire to make a more significant impact drove her to seek the position of Mayor, where she spent an impressive 12 years championing the interests of small and rural communities. During her tenure as Mayor, she engaged directly with Provincial and Federal Governments to address crucial issues facing these communities.

Sharon created Team BC, an innovative program that celebrated the splendor of small and rural communities across British Columbia. It became a resounding success with all 82 BC communities participating, highlighting their unique attributes and putting northern and rural BC on the map.

Advocating at the Provincial Level — UBCM

Recognizing the need for infrastructure development, Sharon represented small communities as the Small Community Representative at UBCM (Union of BC Municipalities). She successfully lobbied for a new grant process that transformed the relationship between smaller communities and the Provincial and Federal Governments — shifting the dynamic from dependency to genuine partnership.

Healthcare & Transportation Leadership

Sharon has always been a champion of healthcare and infrastructure. As Chair of the North West Regional Hospital District, her leadership contributed to improvements in healthcare delivery, including the construction of a new hospital in Masset. To address the critical need for transportation to medical appointments, she initiated the Northern Health Connections Bus — a program that provides vital transportation for medical appointments and post-hospital discharge across the region.

National Advocacy — Federation of Canadian Municipalities

Recognizing the importance of representation at the national level, Sharon was elected to the Federation of Canadian Municipalities (FCM). There, she actively participated in the Rural Forum and successfully advocated for changes in the infrastructure formula for rural communities across Canada.

Her commitment to safety and infrastructure led to her appointment as Vice-Chair of Transportation and Infrastructure for FCM, overseeing airport-related matters, and she also served on the Safety Committee representing Canadian communities on rail safety issues. As a director on the FCM Board, she served on the Environment Committee, exploring innovative technological solutions to benefit communities.

In the Legislature

Elected MLA for Bulkley Valley-Stikine in October 2024, Sharon brings over 20 years of public service to the legislature. As the Official Opposition Critic for Rural Communities and Rural Development, she ensures the voices of northern and rural British Columbians are heard at every level of the legislative process — holding the government accountable on healthcare access, highway maintenance, resource sector policy, and the programs rural families depend on.

Sharon is committed to maintaining an open, non-partisan office that serves every resident of Bulkley Valley-Stikine regardless of how they voted.

Note: Critic portfolio titles may change with legislature session assignments. Contact the office for the most current information.

Key Priorities

  • Improving healthcare access, physician recruitment, and hospital infrastructure across rural BC
  • Adequate highway maintenance funding for Highways 16, 37, and rural roads throughout the riding
  • Supporting forestry, mining, and resource industries that sustain northern communities
  • Expanding internet and cellular connectivity for rural and remote communities
  • Advocating for seniors' services, long-term care, and home support in the north
  • First Nations partnerships, consultation, and meaningful reconciliation
  • Economic development opportunities that keep young people in northern BC

"Throughout her 20-year journey in public service, Sharon has consistently worked towards building strong and healthy communities. Her unwavering commitment is to be a resounding voice for the people of the Bulkley Valley-Stikine Riding, advocating for positive change and a brighter future for all."
