July 2025 Information

Burn Pile Season — What Rural Landowners Need to Know

Each spring and fall, the province regulates when residents can burn yard waste and debris on rural properties. The rules change based on fire danger ratings, and violations can result in fines — but many residents don't know exactly when burning is permitted or who to call to check.

Here is the practical information: The BC Wildfire Service issues daily fire danger ratings for each region. You can check the current rating for our area at gov.bc.ca/wildfires or by calling 1-888-336-7378. Burning is generally only permitted when the fire danger is Low or Moderate.

If you are unsure whether your local municipality or regional district has additional restrictions, contact your local government office directly. The regional districts of Bulkley-Nechako and Kitimat-Stikine may have their own bylaws.

If you've received a notice or fine you believe is incorrect, call Sharon's office — we can help you understand the process and who to contact.

June 2025 Legislature

Sharon Raises Healthcare Staffing in Question Period

During the spring sitting of the BC Legislature, Sharon raised the ongoing physician and nurse shortage affecting communities in Bulkley Valley-Stikine directly with the Minister of Health.

Sharon asked the government to explain what concrete steps it is taking to attract and retain doctors in rural BC — and specifically what incentives exist for physicians willing to practise in small and remote communities like ours.

The government's response referenced existing rural incentive programs. Sharon's assessment is that these programs are insufficient given the scale of the vacancy problem in the north, and she will continue pressing for more substantial commitments in future sittings.

If you are struggling to find a family doctor, the best first step is to register on the Northern Health patient attachment list. If you've been waiting a long time and need help navigating the system, contact this office.

June 2025 Advocacy

Highway Conditions: How to Report and When to Contact This Office

The office has received a number of calls about road conditions on Highway 16 and Highway 37 — potholes, poor shoulder conditions, and slow responses from maintenance contractors. This is one of the most consistent issues raised by constituents across the riding.

How to report a road problem directly:

  • Online: Report a Road Problem at gov.bc.ca
  • By phone: Contact the Ministry of Transportation district office for your area
  • DriveBC: 5-1-1 for road conditions and closures

When to contact this office: If you've already reported a problem and received no response after a reasonable time, or if a road condition poses ongoing safety risks and the ministry is not acting, call us. Sharon can escalate these issues directly with the ministry and put them on the record in the Legislature.

May 2025 Information

The Provincial Budget: What It Means for Bulkley Valley-Stikine

The BC government tabled its annual budget this spring. Sharon reviewed the full document and has outlined the items most relevant to our riding.

Healthcare: The budget includes funding for rural health infrastructure, but the amount allocated for northern BC falls short of what the region needs to address the physician shortage and aging hospital facilities. Sharon voted against the budget on this basis.

Roads: Highway maintenance funding for the northern interior was increased modestly, but does not yet reflect the actual cost of maintaining the road network in a region of this size and geography.

Rural communities: Some additional funding was announced for rural community infrastructure, including grants for small municipalities. If your community organization is interested in applying for any provincial grant program, contact this office — we can help identify relevant programs and support your application.
