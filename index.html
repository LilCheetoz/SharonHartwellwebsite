About Sharon

Serving Bulkley Valley-Stikine

Sharon Hartwell was elected as the MLA for Bulkley Valley-Stikine in the 2024 provincial election. She serves as the Official Opposition Critic for Rural Communities and Rural Development in the BC Legislature.

Raised in northern BC, Sharon understands the unique challenges and opportunities facing rural communities. Her work in Victoria is driven by a commitment to ensuring every corner of the province receives the attention and investment it deserves.

The Riding

Bulkley Valley-Stikine

Covering a vast area of northwestern BC, the Bulkley Valley-Stikine riding encompasses a diverse landscape of mountains, rivers, and thriving communities. From Smithers to Hazelton to Telegraph Creek, this is one of BC's most spectacular regions.

With communities depending on resource industries, agriculture, and small business, the riding's residents deserve strong representation and a voice in decisions that affect their daily lives.

Key Issues

What Sharon Is Fighting For

These are the problems constituents are raising — and what Sharon is actively pushing for in Victoria on their behalf.

The scale of this riding is enormous. From Smithers to the Yukon border, communities are separated by hundreds of kilometres of road. Bulkley Valley-Stikine carries an infrastructure burden — roads, hospitals, schools, emergency services — that is vastly disproportionate to what provincial funding formulas typically account for. Sharon is fighting to change that.

🏥

Rural Healthcare

Residents are driving hours for specialist appointments and basic procedures. Rural hospitals are understaffed and losing doctors they cannot replace.

Sharon is pushing for:
  • Rural physician and specialist recruitment incentives
  • Sustainable funding for northern hospitals and health centres
  • Improved access to mental health and addictions services
🤝

Indigenous Issues & Reconciliation

First Nations communities face unresolved rights questions, infrastructure gaps, and persistent shortfalls in health and social services that demand real government action — not just words.

Sharon is pushing for:
  • Meaningful government-to-government consultation
  • Basic infrastructure and services for all communities
  • Economic development that respects Indigenous rights
🛣️

Roads & Transportation

Highways 16 and 37 are the lifelines of this riding — yet both are chronically underfunded and poorly maintained. In winter, they are dangerous. Year-round, they are inadequate.

Sharon is pushing for:
  • Increased highway maintenance budgets for northern BC
  • Higher standards for winter road safety and response
  • Better rural transit and transportation options
📡

Rural Infrastructure

Internet dead zones, cellular blackouts, and aging water systems leave rural communities without services the rest of BC takes for granted. Every infrastructure cost is multiplied by distance.

Sharon is pushing for:
  • Rural broadband and cellular expansion across the riding
  • Fairer provincial funding formulas for remote communities
  • Upgrades to aging water, waste, and utility systems
🏫

Schools & Education

Rural schools face teacher shortages, limited course options, and aging buildings. When a small community's school closes, families lose more than a classroom — they lose the centre of their community.

Sharon is pushing for:
  • Rural school funding equity and building upgrades
  • Teacher recruitment and retention for northern BC
  • Protection for small schools at risk of closure
🚒

Emergency Response

Wildfires, floods, and winter storms regularly threaten communities across this riding. Emergency response resources and funding consistently fall short of what northern BC actually needs.

Sharon is pushing for:
  • Better emergency preparedness funding for northern communities
  • Support for volunteer firefighters and first responders
  • Improved regional disaster coordination and early warning
